Account Deletion Information

At Garuda Matrimony, we value your privacy and provide you with full control over your personal data. You can request the deletion of your account and associated profile information through the application at any time.

Warning: Account deletion is PERMANENT. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to your profile and the ability to log in.

How to Request Account Deletion

In-App Deletion

The standard method to delete your account is through the application interface:

  1. Open the Garuda Matrimony app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Navigate to the My Profile section.
  4. Tap the Edit button.
  5. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  6. Tap Delete Account.
  7. Confirm the permanent deletion in the dialog box.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account via the app, the following information is removed from our active production environment:

  • Authentication Account: Your registered login credentials and unique user identifier.
  • Profile Information: All details stored within your primary profile document, including name, age, gender, location, language, bio, and marital status.
  • Profile Photograph: Your uploaded profile image stored in our cloud storage.

Information That May Remain

Certain interaction records are not automatically purged during the account deletion process and may remain in the system:

  • Messages: Existing chat records and messages sent to other users are stored centrally and are not currently removed when an individual account is deleted.
  • Shortlist Records: Records of your profile appearing in other users' shortlists, or shortlist selections made by you, may persist in the system data.

Data Retained for Legal or Security Reasons

In accordance with standard data practices, certain information may be retained for a limited period where necessary for:

  • Security and fraud prevention.
  • Compliance with legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Resolution of disputes.

Some data may also remain temporarily in encrypted backups until those backups are rotated or deleted according to our standard retention procedures.

Processing Time

Account deletion initiated through the app removes the authentication account and profile document as part of the immediate deletion workflow. Verification of requests submitted through other channels may take additional time. Data stored in backups will be cleared upon the completion of the applicable backup retention cycle.
