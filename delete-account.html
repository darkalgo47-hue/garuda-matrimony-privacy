Delete Your Account & Data

At Garuda Matrimony, we respect your right to privacy and your control over your personal data. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated personal information at any time.

Warning: Account deletion is PERMANENT. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to your profile, matches, shortlisted profiles, and chat history. This action cannot be undone.

How to Request Account Deletion

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly through the Garuda Matrimony Android app:

  1. Open the Garuda Matrimony app on your device.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Tap on the My Profile tab in the navigation menu.
  4. Tap the Edit (pencil icon) located at the top right of the screen.
  5. Scroll to the bottom of the page to find the "Danger Zone" section.
  6. Tap the Delete Account button.
  7. Confirm your decision in the popup dialog to permanently remove your data.

Option 2: Email Request

If you can no longer access the application, you can request deletion via email:

  • Send an email to: [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]
  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request - Garuda Matrimony
  • Please include the email address associated with your account for verification purposes.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon successful processing of your deletion request, the following information will be permanently removed from our active production systems:

  • Account Identity: Your name, registered email address, and authentication identifiers.
  • Matrimony Profile: All personal details including age, gender, location, language, and marital status.
  • Media: All profile photographs and images stored in our cloud storage.
  • Interactions: Your chat history and messages sent to other users.
  • Preferences: Your partner preferences and saved/shortlisted profiles.

Data That May Be Retained

While we aim to delete all data, certain information may be retained for a limited period under the following circumstances:

  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.
  • Security & Fraud: To prevent fraud, ensure app security, and protect our users.
  • Dispute Resolution: For the resolution of any ongoing legal disputes or issues.
  • Backups: Data may persist in encrypted server backups for a limited time until the backup is rotated or deleted per our standard procedures.

Retained data is stored only for the duration required for its specific purpose and is subsequently deleted or anonymized.

Deletion Processing Time

Account deletion requests initiated through the app are processed immediately. Email requests are typically processed within a reasonable period (usually 7-14 business days) after we have verified your identity. Residual data in system backups will be cleared according to our standard backup rotation cycle.
