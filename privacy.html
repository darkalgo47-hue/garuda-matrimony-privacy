Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Garuda Matrimony ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account & Profile Information

  • Personal Details: Name, age, gender, language, location, pincode, and marital status.
  • Photos: Profile pictures uploaded by the user.
  • Bio: Personal description provided by the user.

Authentication

We use Firebase Authentication to manage user accounts and secure sign-ins.

Communications

Chat: We collect and store chat messages sent between users to facilitate communication.

Advertising

AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID) to provide personalized or non-personalized advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your user profile.
  • To enable matching and communication with other users.
  • To display relevant advertisements.
  • To improve our services and user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage.

4. Data Deletion

Users can request account and data deletion through the application settings or by contacting support.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase: For authentication, database, and storage.
  • AdMob: For advertising services.
